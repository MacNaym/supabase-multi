# Code of Conduct

We want Supabase Multi to be a useful, technical, low-drama open-source project.

Be respectful, stay on topic, criticize ideas and code rather than people, and do not harass contributors or users.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, doxxing, deliberate credential exposure, spam, and repeated disruptive behavior.

Project maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove content and may restrict participation when necessary to protect contributors and users.

For conduct concerns, use the private project contact listed in `SECURITY.md` until a dedicated moderation contact is published.
